## Wi-Fi Devices and Their Capabilities (Example)

Below are typical day-to-day devices, their wireless properties, and corresponding Wi-Fi generations:

---

### 1. Smartphone (e.g., modern Android/iPhone)
- **Supported Bands:** 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, 6 GHz  
- **Wi-Fi Standard:** IEEE 802.11ax  
- **Max Data Rate:** Up to ~1–2 Gbps (device dependent)  
- **Features:**
  - MU-MIMO
  - OFDMA
  - Target Wake Time (TWT)
- **Wi-Fi Generation:** **Wi-Fi 6 / Wi-Fi 6E**

---

### 2. Laptop (e.g., recent Windows/Linux laptop)
- **Supported Bands:** 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz (sometimes 6 GHz)  
- **Wi-Fi Standard:** IEEE 802.11ac or 802.11ax  
- **Max Data Rate:** ~433 Mbps to 1.2 Gbps  
- **Features:**
  - MIMO
  - Beamforming
- **Wi-Fi Generation:**  
  - **Wi-Fi 5 (802.11ac)** or  
  - **Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax)**

---

### 3. Smart TV
- **Supported Bands:** Mostly 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz  
- **Wi-Fi Standard:** IEEE 802.11n / 802.11ac  
- **Max Data Rate:** ~150–866 Mbps  
- **Features:**
  - Basic MIMO support
- **Wi-Fi Generation:**  
  - **Wi-Fi 4 (802.11n)** or  
  - **Wi-Fi 5 (802.11ac)**

---

### 4. Wi-Fi Router (Home Router)
- **Supported Bands:** Dual-band or Tri-band (2.4 / 5 / 6 GHz)  
- **Wi-Fi Standard:** IEEE 802.11ac or 802.11ax  
- **Max Data Rate:** Up to several Gbps (combined)  
- **Features:**
  - MU-MIMO
  - OFDMA (Wi-Fi 6)
  - Beamforming
- **Wi-Fi Generation:**  
  - **Wi-Fi 5 (802.11ac)** or  
  - **Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax)**

---

### 5. HikVision CCTV Camera System
- **Supported Bands:** Typically 2.4 GHz (some models support 5 GHz)  
- **Wi-Fi Standard:** IEEE 802.11b/g/n  
- **Max Data Rate:** ~11–150 Mbps  
- **Features:**
  - Stable long-range connectivity
  - Optimized for continuous video streaming
  - Low-power wireless modules
- **Wi-Fi Generation:**  
  - **Wi-Fi 3 (802.11g)** or  
  - **Wi-Fi 4 (802.11n)**

## Conclusion
Different devices support different Wi-Fi generations based on their hardware capabilities.  
Modern devices tend to use **Wi-Fi 6/6E (802.11ax)** for higher speed and efficiency, while surveillance and embedded systems like CCTV cameras often rely on **Wi-Fi 4 (802.11n)** for stability and range.
